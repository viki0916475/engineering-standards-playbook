# Delivery Metrics

## Overview

Delivery metrics provide measurable insight into engineering performance, delivery predictability, and operational effectiveness.

The objective is to improve flow, reduce bottlenecks, and support sustainable delivery practices across engineering teams.

## Core Metrics

### Cycle Time

Measures the time taken from work starting to production delivery.

Focus areas:

- Reducing delays
- Improving flow efficiency
- Identifying bottlenecks
- Supporting predictable delivery

### Throughput

Measures the amount of work completed within a given period.

Used to:

- Understand delivery capacity
- Support release planning
- Improve forecasting accuracy

### Work In Progress (WIP)

Tracks the volume of active work across teams.

Objectives:

- Reduce context switching
- Improve focus
- Minimise delivery delays
- Support smoother flow

## Supporting Metrics

Additional indicators may include:

- Deployment frequency
- Lead time
- Change failure rate
- Escaped defects
- Reliability metrics
- Incident response times
- Platform availability

## Engineering Leadership Focus

Metrics should support continuous improvement rather than individual performance monitoring.

Effective use of metrics enables:

- Better delivery predictability
- Improved engineering maturity
- Increased transparency
- Faster issue resolution
- Data-driven decision making

## Flow Review Approach

Regular flow reviews should focus on:

- Delivery bottlenecks
- Team dependencies
- Release risks
- Capacity constraints
- Process inefficiencies

## Continuous Improvement

Metrics should evolve alongside organisational maturity and delivery objectives.

The goal is sustainable, scalable, and resilient software delivery.
