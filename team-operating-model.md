# Team Operating Model

## Engineering Structure

The engineering operating model is designed to support scalable SaaS delivery across multiple Scrum teams operating within fast-paced product environments.

Typical structure:

- Engineering Director / Head of Engineering
- Engineering Managers
- Technical Leads
- Scrum Teams
- QA / QE
- Platform & DevOps
- Architecture & Product Collaboration

## Delivery Model

Teams operate using Agile / SCRUM methodologies with a strong focus on:

- Predictable delivery
- Continuous improvement
- Clear ownership
- Incremental value delivery
- Engineering quality standards
- Cross-functional collaboration

## Team Distribution

Engineering teams may operate across:

- Onshore teams
- Nearshore teams
- Offshore teams

Key principles include:

- Shared standards and governance
- Consistent engineering practices
- Strong communication cadence
- Unified tooling and CI/CD processes
- Transparent delivery metrics

## Scrum Team Structure

Typical Scrum team composition:

- Technical Lead
- Backend Engineers
- Frontend Engineers
- QA / QE Engineers
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master (where applicable)

## Delivery Governance

Delivery governance includes:

- Sprint planning
- Backlog refinement
- Architecture reviews
- Release governance
- Flow metrics reviews
- Incident and reliability reviews
- Continuous improvement sessions

## Engineering Leadership Focus

Leadership priorities include:

- Building high-performing teams
- Coaching engineering managers and technical leads
- Removing delivery bottlenecks
- Supporting career growth and succession planning
- Encouraging innovation and technical excellence
- Promoting accountability and ownership

## Collaboration Model

Engineering works closely with:

- Product strategy
- Architecture
- Design
- Security
- Platform engineering
- Business stakeholders

to ensure successful and scalable product delivery.
